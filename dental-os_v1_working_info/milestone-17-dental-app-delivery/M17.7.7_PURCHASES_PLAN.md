## Scope, page, and workflow

Internal module `purchases` at `finance/purchases`. Show month-filtered supplier invoices with search by supplier or invoice number. Record product or lab-service purchases with supplier, date, invoice number, line items, discount, and note; review before saving; open purchase details; correct or void existing records. Product and supplier quick-create actions remain within their respective Finance domains. The pinned form submits no initial supplier payment; supplier payments remain a separate source record.

## Business rules and permissions

- Purchases and their supplier/product reference reads require `finance.manage` (admin).
- A purchase uses an active registered supplier and active default clinic branch. Types are `product` and `lab_service`. Line item, quantity, unit, rate, invoice, discount, and date validation stay aligned with the Worker.
- Worker computes stored line/subtotal/discount/total amounts. The client may preview form arithmetic, but stored totals, supplier balances, and any report totals remain Worker-owned.
- Invoice number uniqueness is per supplier; surface duplicate-risk warnings and handle Worker conflicts. A correction requires a reason and creates a replacement while retaining the original; a void requires a reason and only affects a current recorded purchase.
- Every create/correct/void request has an idempotency key. Do not split or combine supplier payment writes with purchase writes; the SaaS Purchase form records the invoice only.

## API requirements

- `GET, POST /v1/clinic/purchases`.
- `POST /v1/clinic/purchases/:id/correct` with a full replacement and reason.
- `POST /v1/clinic/purchases/:id/void` with a reason.
- `GET /v1/clinic/products` supplies product choices; `GET /v1/clinic/suppliers` supplies supplier choices. All listed routes are already in the Finance SDK allowlist.
- Purchase, product, and supplier list routes are not server-paginated. Confirm the valid maximum purchase payload remains within the SDK's 12 KiB JSON request bound; if it does not, request a reviewed contract change.
