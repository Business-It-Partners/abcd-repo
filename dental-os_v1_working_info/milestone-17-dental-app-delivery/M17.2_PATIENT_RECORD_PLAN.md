## Patient Record UI and workflow source

The user requirement is to carry the Dental SaaS Patient Record UI/UX across without inventing replacement screens. The app now renders the pinned production `PatientRecordView`, `CaseEntryView`, `PatientDrawer` and detail tabs, plus the original shared UI components, date controls and stylesheets. Its page wrapper follows the production `clinicApp` → `clinicWorkspaceCanvas` → `clinicCareWorkspace` structure. The former custom Patient Record views and stylesheet have been removed.

A byte comparison against the pinned production revision found 121 of 128 vendored files identical. Seven files are adapted only at platform seams for Dental OS SDK networking, private media, in-memory routing, session role and patient-scoped media IDs; they do not replace visible view markup or CSS. `SOURCE_IMPORT.json` records the full adapter list, source blob IDs and comparison result.

The app preserves the active SaaS screens and maps their callbacks to the existing Dental OS `ClinicClient` and media SDK. The Worker remains the business authority; no renderer credentials, direct networking, local authoritative database or offline write queue were introduced.

## App capabilities and platform limits

- Patient directory, production registration form, patient profile and safety data, cases, notes, timeline, treatment plan, periodontal/odontogram/canal records, laboratory tabs, X-rays, prescriptions, follow-ups and capability-gated billing are presented by the pinned SaaS views.
- Existing Dental OS SDK routes enforce current role and row-version contracts. Unknown write outcomes remain retryable with the original operation identity.
- Patient image viewing and uploads use the host media broker. Image withdrawal/deletion is unavailable because the current app media SDK exposes read/upload only. The pinned source's legacy inactive `TimelineByCase` amendment callbacks have no corresponding active route or Worker write API.
- Native print/export and cross-app navigation are not added as platform capabilities.
