## Scope, page, and workflow

Internal module `transaction-record` at `finance/transactions`. Recreate the read-only Transaction Record page: show Worker report rows, cash-in/cash-out/net totals and Worker-produced Finance overview; filter the report by the current clinic month or all dates. The month picker uses the SaaS Bikram Sambat month selection and sends its inclusive AD start/end range. Preserve the SaaS category labels and the distinction between patient payment, operating expense, salary payout, and supplier payment. Purchases are invoices, not cash movement, so do not add invoice totals to cash-out a second time.

## Business rules and permission

This is an admin Finance view guarded by `finance.manage`. The Worker derives the report from the authenticated clinic database. Keep its totals and outstanding-balance projections authoritative; never recalculate authoritative totals from displayed or partially loaded rows. Validate ordered date ranges; show the Worker result as returned.

## API requirements

- `GET /v1/clinic/transaction-record` with optional `from` and `to` date query values. The Finance-family SDK route already exists and requires `finance.manage`.
- Response: `report.transactions`, `cashInTotal`, `cashOutTotal`, `netCashAmount`, and `overview` (patient due, general expenses, product purchases, supplier payouts, salary payouts, and current supplier/lab due fields).
- Use the `clinic.api.request` grant with the same payload/query limits as the current SDK. There are no writes in this child.

## Dependencies and states

Requires the Finance parent shell, trusted module metadata, shared SDK connection/session context, and the existing M15 Finance family route. The report list is not server-paginated; date range is the only list bound and the SDK response ceiling is 256 KiB. Prove representative reports fit, or resolve an approved bounded/report contract before claiming history support.

Show initial loading, refresh busy state, invalid period, API/permission/connection errors, and an empty period with zero Worker totals. Keep the last successful result clearly distinguished from a failed refresh.
