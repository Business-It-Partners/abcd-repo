## Scope, page, and workflow

Internal module `supplier-payments` at `finance/supplier-payments`. Show all active supplier-payment records with clinic-month/all-month filter. Record, correct, and void a payment, optionally associate it with a purchase, and refresh supplier statements after writes. This is the dedicated payment directory; the Supplier list child separately exposes the SaaS Record payment action from supplier detail.

## Business rules and permissions

- The Finance module and payment list/correction/void routes require `finance.manage` (admin). The Worker also has a restricted `lab_tracker.access` alternative for create-only Lab Tracker use: lab scope, no linked purchase, and a supplier typed Lab. This Finance module exposes the Finance-managed workflow and does not claim or add Lab Tracker access.
- Payment date must be valid; amount is positive NPR with at most two decimals; methods are cash, eSewa, Khalti, card, bank transfer, or cheque. Scope is `general` or `lab`. Supplier must be active and an active default branch must exist.
- An optional purchase ID must identify a purchase belonging to the selected supplier. Product invoices require general scope; lab-service invoices require lab scope. The Worker defines the associated-purchase validation; do not invent an overpayment limit absent from this production service contract.
- Correction and void require a reason, only apply to a currently recorded row, retain the original audit history, and conflict if the row changed. Create/correction/void use idempotency receipts and same-key replay semantics.
- Worker remains authority for supplier due, cash out, status, audit, and transaction projections.

## API requirements

- `GET, POST /v1/clinic/supplier-payments`.
- `POST /v1/clinic/supplier-payments/:id/correct` and `POST /v1/clinic/supplier-payments/:id/void`.
- Read dependencies: `GET /v1/clinic/suppliers`, `GET /v1/clinic/purchases`, and `GET /v1/clinic/suppliers/:id/statement` after each successful payment write to reconcile the affected supplier account.
- Existing Finance SDK route rules cover these methods; this Finance module is gated by `finance.manage`. The Worker’s separate restricted Lab Tracker create path remains Worker-owned. The payment list is an unpaginated full array.
