## Scope, page, and workflow

Internal module `supplier-list` at `finance/suppliers`. Display the shared supplier directory, add/edit and active-status workflows, route-backed supplier detail/back navigation, and the Worker-provided supplier statement. Supplier detail includes opening balance, purchases, payments, current due, and a Record payment action. That detail action is part of this SaaS Supplier list sub-app and creates the same supplier-payment record consumed by the later Supplier payments sub-app.

## Business rules and permissions

- Supplier list, updates, statement, and payment creation require `finance.manage` (admin).
- Supplier types are goods, lab, or both. Preserve contact person, phone, email, address, opening-balance amount/date, and active state. Supplier updates use `expectedRowVersion` and reject stale edits.
- The Worker calculates current due from opening balance and recorded purchases/payments, separating general and lab scopes. Use the statement projection as the balance authority; if it fails, show an error and omit the balance instead of summing a partial local list.
- Payment created from detail uses an active supplier, valid date, positive NPR amount, valid method/scope, and idempotency key. Worker validates related purchase ownership/scope if one is supplied.
- Do not allow supplier list UI to mutate an invoice or payment record without the Worker correction/void contract.

## API requirements

- `GET, POST /v1/clinic/suppliers` and `PATCH /v1/clinic/suppliers/:id`.
- `GET /v1/clinic/suppliers/:id/statement` for the authoritative statement.
- Detail's Record payment action uses `POST /v1/clinic/supplier-payments`; response schema and scope validation are the same as M17.7.8.
- The pinned detail page also reads `GET /v1/clinic/purchases` for its Purchases tab, purchase detail, and optional payment association, and `GET /v1/clinic/supplier-payments` for statement payment details. These read routes stay within `finance.manage`; they do not add Purchases or Supplier payments workflows to this child.
- Finance-family route rules already exist. Supplier directory and statement endpoints return full arrays/projections without cursor pagination.

## Dependencies and important states

Requires the Finance shell, Finance capability, Products and Supplier domain mapping, and supplier-payment create contract. It precedes Purchases because purchases require a registered supplier. It also gives the later Supplier payments child a registered supplier catalogue and stable supplier IDs. Keep statement loading separate from directory loading and handle route repair when a selected supplier disappears.

Show directory loading/error/empty/no-filter-match, add/edit modal validation/save error, optimistic active-toggle busy/failure, opening supplier details, statement loading/failure, empty statement, and record-payment modal state. Preserve opening balances and all historical rows on refresh.
