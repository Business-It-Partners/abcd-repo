# M17.4 Plan — Work Entry

**Status:** PLANNED. App source has not yet been imported into M17.
**Package:** company.dental.work-entry

## App scope

Bring over the SaaS clinical encounter workflow: chief complaint and notes, treatment stages and plan, billing/lab references where available, review and commit, and the source-defined post-commit state.

## SDK and acceptance focus

- Inventory exact SaaS Worker routes, methods, fields, permissions, and multi-step write semantics before wiring the app. Use the Dental OS SDK for every business call.
- Preserve one stable operation identity across the encounter commit. Verify duplicate-submit prevention, lost-response/unknown reconciliation, conflict handling, and refresh of the resulting record.
- Use synthetic patients and treatment data; test correct role and clinic scope, denial cases, and audit behavior.
- Verify validation, unsaved-work protection, retry/recovery copy, keyboard/focus, and managed-window lifecycle.
- Do not add direct Patient Record navigation or a general intent/message bus. Any cross-app destination remains gated by its own approved platform contract.

The M17 implementation must not silently split an intended atomic clinical action into independent writes unless the maintained SaaS contract specifies that behavior.
