## Pinned business contract

The production SaaS workflow document is `docs/product/workflows/treatment-and-work-entry.md`; the atomic-write decision is `docs/decisions/records/ADR-0002-atomic-open-case-work-entry.md`. The main open-case form is one in-memory draft. Its final `POST /v1/work-entry/commit` carries `expectedCaseRowVersion` and all non-media draft changes. The Worker runs `parseWorkEntryCommitInput`, checks `work_entry.access` (and `patient_payments.collect` if payment is present), passes the idempotency key to `runPatientCommand`, and commits the D1 effects in one batch. Do not reproduce this fan-out in the renderer.

The host recognizes `clinic.app.work-entry`, enforces the `work_entry.access` family policy, and has an explicit Work Entry API route allowlist. Follow-up reads remain guarded by `followups.access`, and patient-ledger reads by `patient_billing_ledger.read`; the Worker is the final authority. In the initial 1.0.0–1.0.5 releases, patient-media upload/withdrawal was unavailable to Work Entry. Signed 1.0.6 adds exactly `clinic.media.read` and `clinic.media.upload`; private image listing/withdrawal remains through the authorized `ClinicClient` routes, and image bytes go only through the OS media bridge. Do not use app-owned HTTP or change backend code/data.

Principal route surface in the pinned source:

- Read: `GET /v1/work-entry/cases`, patient lookup/core, staff references, treatment protocols, case work-entry, odontogram/periodontal/canal data, follow-up list when permitted, supplier/lab references, and patient ledger when permitted.
- Separate explicit case commands: create case/complaint; update case; add/cancel a protocol or stage; reopen/complete/update status; and finalize the source's distinct completed-work flow.
- Atomic open-case commit: `POST /v1/work-entry/commit`. The Worker supports plan selections and stage completion/additions, note, odontogram, periodontal chart, canal readings, prescription, lab orders, follow-up, optional payment, and explicit close disposition in the one command.
- Every business request goes through `ClinicClient`/the host broker. No credential getter, arbitrary fetch, direct Worker/R2 request, local authoritative store, or hidden write queue.

## SDK and acceptance focus

- Inventory exact SaaS Worker routes, methods, fields, permissions, and multi-step write semantics before wiring the app. Use the Dental OS SDK for every business call.
- Preserve one stable operation identity across the encounter commit. Verify duplicate-submit prevention, lost-response/unknown reconciliation, conflict handling, and refresh of the resulting record.
- Use synthetic patients and treatment data; test correct role and clinic scope, denial cases, and audit behavior.
- Verify validation, unsaved-work protection, retry/recovery copy, keyboard/focus, and managed-window lifecycle.
- Keep the serialized command and its idempotency identity stable for same-command recovery after an unknown result; block a second commit until the original result is resolved. Refresh the case and ledger after a confirmed commit.
- Do not add direct Patient Record navigation or a general intent/message bus. Any cross-app destination remains gated by its own approved platform contract.

The M17 implementation must not silently split an intended atomic clinical action into independent writes unless the maintained SaaS contract specifies that behavior.
