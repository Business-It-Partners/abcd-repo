## Scope, page, and workflow

Internal module `salary-payments` at `finance/salary-payouts`. Load the salary payout history and active staff directory, choose a clinic salary month, record a payout, and correct or void an existing payout with an auditable reason. Preserve the source fields: staff, pay period, payout type, base amount, bonus amount, paid date, payment method/reference, and note.

## Business rules and permissions

- Salary endpoints require `finance.manage`. The staff directory call `GET /v1/clinic/staff` additionally requires `staff.manage`; admin has both. Do not request the broader Staff app family.
- The Worker accepts payout types advance, salary, adjustment, or other, and payment methods cash, bank transfer, or other.
- Staff must be active and an active default clinic branch must exist. Pay period is a validated `YYYY-MM` string; paid date must be a valid `YYYY-MM-DD` date. Base and bonus are non-negative NPR amounts, each with at most two decimal places; their sum must be positive and at most NPR 100,000,000.
- A correction supplies a full replacement and reason, and may choose a different active staff member. Void/correction applies only to a currently recorded row and conflicts if stale/already changed. Both paths preserve audit history and require idempotency.
- Salary records and reports are server-owned. Do not calculate authoritative salary totals from local staff profiles or displayed rows.

## API requirements

- `GET /v1/clinic/salary-payouts` and `POST /v1/clinic/salary-payouts`.
- `POST /v1/clinic/salary-payouts/:id/correct` and `POST /v1/clinic/salary-payouts/:id/void`.
- `GET /v1/clinic/staff` for active staff and current salary profile fields; require both the Finance app family and `staff.manage`.
- The Finance-family route allowlist already includes these methods. Salary and staff lists are full arrays without server pagination.

## Dependencies and important states

Requires the Finance shell and M15 generic JSON SDK, with the additional trusted `staff.manage` claim on the staff request. Handle simultaneous salary/staff loading, staff load failure, no active staff, salary month/all month, no records, list error, add/edit form validation, save/correction error, void reason modal, conflict, and unknown write response. Treat base salary profile values as defaults only; a recorded payout's submitted values are the source.
