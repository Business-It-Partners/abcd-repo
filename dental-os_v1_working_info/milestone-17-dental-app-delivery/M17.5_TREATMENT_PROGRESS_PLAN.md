# M17.5 Plan — Treatment Progress

**Status:** PLANNED. App source has not yet been imported into M17.
**Package:** company.dental.treatment-progress

## App scope

Bring over the current SaaS treatment-stage aggregation and patient-scoped progress views, including completed and pending work, refresh behavior, and source-defined filters or summaries.

## SDK and acceptance focus

- Use the Dental OS SDK and current SaaS routes; verify server-enforced clinic/patient scoping and permitted roles.
- Confirm displayed stage status and totals against synthetic source records, including empty, partially complete, completed, corrected, and stale data.
- Exercise refresh, denied access, Worker errors, bounded lists, and any source-defined conflict state.
- Preserve app/window boundaries. Do not implement an app-to-app navigation mechanism; patient destinations remain unavailable unless a separately approved OS contract is in place.
- Verify keyboard/focus, useful loading/error/empty states, and Store install/update.

No local treatment database or independently inferred clinical status is in scope.
