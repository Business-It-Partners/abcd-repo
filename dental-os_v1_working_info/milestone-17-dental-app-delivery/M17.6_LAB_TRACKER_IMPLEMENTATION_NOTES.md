## Implemented locally

- Imported and adapted the SaaS lab order list, status timeline/detail drawer, pending assignment form, lab work options, BS month picker, supplier directory, and supplier order history.
- Added a standalone M17 package with two same-package modules: `lab-tracker` and `lab-suppliers`.
- Reads lab orders and lab-scoped suppliers through the M15 `ClinicClient`; writes assignment, order progress/notes/cancellation and supplier changes as idempotent commands.
- Uses optimistic `rowVersion` fields on every write. An unknown write result retains its original command identity for retry; a conflict reloads clinic data.
- Keeps clinical request details read-only after submission, edits reception notes separately, retains cancellation reason/history, and does not offer restoration or invalid cancellation for pending/fitted orders.
- Keeps Lab Supplier Record operational: contact details and lab order history only. It uses the shared Finance supplier record and requires `finance.manage` to change a supplier shared with Finance.
- Added app contract tests and a private synthetic Electron fixture covering assignment, progression, notes, cancellation, supplier creation/history, module selection and role denial.
