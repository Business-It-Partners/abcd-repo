# M17.3 Plan — Appointments

**Status:** PLANNED. App source has not yet been imported into M17.
**Package:** company.dental.appointments

## App scope

Bring over the SaaS appointment workflow: bounded date/bucket and doctor reads; patient association; create, edit/reschedule, and permitted status changes; calendar/list state; and source-defined validation and recovery.

## SDK and acceptance focus

- Confirm current SaaS routes, method/query contracts, role rules, row revisions, and payload sizes; route calls only through the Dental OS SDK.
- Verify booking, edit/reschedule, status transitions, overlap/conflict behavior, stale revisions, refresh, and safe retry/reconciliation for uncertain writes.
- Use synthetic staging patients, doctors, and appointments. Test role and tenant denials without touching real records.
- Verify date boundaries, loading/empty/error states, dirty drafts, keyboard/focus, and app-only Store install/update.

Historical M09 appointment API evidence is not M17 app acceptance. M17.3 needs app-package evidence against its exact current source and the managed OS broker.
