# M17.9 Plan — Staff

**Status:** PLANNED. App source has not yet been imported into M17.
**Package:** company.dental.staff

## App scope

Bring over staff directory/profile, permitted staff edits, work history, and salary history from the maintained SaaS module. Preserve role-specific tabs and data visibility.

## SDK and acceptance focus

- Derive the exact route and permission matrix from SaaS and the Dental OS SDK. Verify directory, profile, edit, work-history, and salary-history visibility for authorized roles and denials for others.
- Integrate private Staff documents through the M15 media SDK/host transfer path only. Do not expose bearer tokens, signed URLs, or direct cloud requests to app code.
- Verify clinic isolation, document read/upload integrity as supported, size/type errors, session change, and audit/refresh using synthetic data.
- Keep file state, retention, withdrawal, and deletion owned by SaaS. Do not add OS/app file deletion or retention behavior.
- Verify staff-edit idempotency/conflicts, dirty forms, keyboard/focus, and Store install/update.

Document workflows that lack a confirmed SDK grant or Worker rule as blocked; do not create broader app permissions to make the UI appear complete.
