## M15.9 implementation update — 2026-10-25

In `/home/master-x/.codex/worktrees/dental-os-audit/fewa_business_os` on `codex/m15-readiness`, M15.9 adds a 10-second main-process resource monitor. The policy measures Linux `MemAvailable` (or the platform memory signal) and aggregate managed app-renderer working set. For available RAM, warning is `max(768 MiB, 15% of total)` capped at 25%, and critical is `max(384 MiB, 8% of total)` capped at 12%. For managed app-renderer working set, warning is `max(512 MiB, 12% of total)` capped at 1.5 GiB, and critical is `max(768 MiB, 20% of total)` capped at 2 GiB. Ordinary pressure requires two 10-second samples; severe critical pressure acts immediately; three normal samples re-arm the policy.

The shell closes the App Store window and unmounts its React content before the main process reclaims eligible app renderers. Warning pressure reclaims non-visible clean apps; critical pressure may also pause a visible clean app. Apps with unsaved work, a modal, pending/unknown writes, or unsafe broker writes remain open. Paused apps can be reopened after the measured system pressure clears. The shell renderer, main-process login/session authority, and the separate shell recovery surface are not cleanup candidates. Because the App Store is a managed window inside the shell renderer, this releases its UI subtree but does not claim a separate Store process exit.
